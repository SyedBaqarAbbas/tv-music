# Project requirements

Any program developed here must be efficient and run easily on laptops with 8 GB RAM or less.

- Keep the Android app small and use the platform SDK where practical.
- Keep Gradle at one worker, a 1 GB JVM heap, and no persistent daemon.
- Prefer a physical TV and the offline browser preview to an Android emulator.
- Avoid continuous work while lyrics are hidden or the activity is paused.
- Treat TV memory/frame-time figures as targets until measured on hardware.
